# Community code of conduct

TrinityMatrix welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience,
and viewpoints. Be respectful, make feedback specific, and discuss ideas and
behavior without attacking people. Assume good intent while remaining open
to correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation,
publication of private information, and repeated disruptive conduct are not
acceptable. Do not post another person's account details, emails, or game data.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or
restrict participation when needed. Enforcement should be proportionate and
based on observable behavior. Affected contributors may request reconsideration
through a private channel agreed with the maintainer.

For sensitive conduct reports, ask the repository owner for a private contact
route without posting the sensitive details publicly. Maintainers should
handle reports discreetly and disclose information only as needed to respond.
Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
